Part of every ticket goes to a humanitarian project. This concert's goal: to help children and families in Kosovo and Metohija and to contribute to the restoration of St. Nicholas Church in Lyons.

What to expect Шта вас очекује

An evening of old songs

Traditional two-part harmonies, epic ballads, wedding songs and seasonal melodies, sung in traditional dress with live accompaniment. Between songs we tell you where each one comes from and what it means.

Part of every ticket goes to the concert's humanitarian project. Read more on the blog.

Where do I buy tickets?

Online through TicketsCandy. Use any "Buy tickets" button on this website.

When does the concert start?

The concert starts at 5:00 PM on Sunday, December 20, and ends around 7:00 PM.

Can children come?

Yes. Our concerts are for the whole family.

Can we book Zlatni Vez for an event?

Yes. Write to us through the contact page with the date, place and type of event.
